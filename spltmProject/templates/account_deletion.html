Account & Data Deletion Request

At SplitMoney, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data. You may request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal information at any time.

Important: Due to financial compliance and audit requirements, certain transaction records may be retained as required by law, even after account deletion.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your SplitMoney account:

  • Email us at support@splitmoney.example
  • Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  • Send the request from your registered email ID or include your registered mobile number

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • User profile information (name, email, mobile number)
  • Authentication credentials
  • Profile image
  • Associated event participation records (where legally permitted)
  • Organizer wallet display data

What Data May Be Retained

In compliance with financial regulations, audit requirements, fraud prevention policies, and dispute resolution processes, we may retain:

  • Transaction records
  • Payment gateway reference IDs
  • Vendor payout records
  • Audit logs related to financial activities

Retained data will be securely archived and will not be used for marketing purposes.

Processing Time

Deletion requests are processed within 7 working days after identity verification.
